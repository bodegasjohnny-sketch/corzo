Overall Upgrade

Agentic layer for Chris Corzo Injury Attorneys — from 7-question quiz to 24/7 voice, chronology, and content engine.

Current Stack Pain Points

  • 7-question case evaluation is pure lead capture. Visitor gets no value; completion is okay but quality is low.
  • No document intelligence. Medical records and crash reports still require manual review after the lead comes in.
  • Intake is human-bound. After-hours and high-volume moments create lag.
  • Social content is manual and inconsistent. Local presence is strong but not systematically fueled by case patterns.
  • No agentic loop connecting qualification → analysis → content → next lead.

1. Upgraded 7-Step Case Evaluation

Same short length, same mobile speed, but every answer now feeds structured data.

1
Accident type & date
Sets jurisdiction timing and comparative-fault window.
2
Vehicle type (car / commercial truck / other)
Routes to the right investigation path and insurance complexity.
3
Injuries & treatment so far
ER, MRI, surgery, PT — feeds chronology and specials later.
4
Current pain / limitations
Captures ongoing damages language for demand packages.
5
Fault indicators
Police report, citations, witnesses — early liability signals.
6
Insurance / commercial indicators
Flags deeper coverage and potential additional defendants.
7
Contact + optional upload
Name, phone, and the option to drop a PDF (records or crash report). This is where the chronology agent activates.

Why it matters: Friction stays low. Lead quality and downstream automation rise because the answers are structured instead of free-text.

2. Voice Agent (Not Just Chat)

In-browser or phone voice that walks the same 7 points conversationally. Works after hours, for people who prefer talking, and in high-volume moments.

Outputs the identical structured case object the form produces, so the rest of the system doesn’t care how the lead arrived.

Why it matters: Many injury leads bounce on forms. Voice captures them 24/7 without adding headcount.

3. Automated Video Pipeline

From structured case patterns (or anonymized past results) the system generates short-form scripts and assets:

  • “What a 54-day treatment gap does to a case”
  • “3 red flags from an MRI + PT sequence”
  • Local Louisiana highway / truck scenes with plain-English explainers
  • Client-style stories with proper disclaimers

Ready for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.

Why it matters: Turns every good case pattern into consistent local content without the partners filming every week.

4. Agentic Layer (24/7 Loop)

  1. Case eval (form or voice) produces structured data.
  2. Chronology + red-flag + specials agent runs (the same engine already live on this site).
  3. Prospect receives an instant report; firm receives a clean handoff.
  4. Nightly or on-demand content generation pulls from the same patterns.
  5. Simple dashboard shows new leads, gaps flagged, and posts ready to publish.

Why it matters: Humans only touch high-value decisions. The heavy lifting runs continuously.

5. Local Scaling on Top of the Agents

  • Parish-specific landing pages that reuse the same voice + chronology engine.
  • “Upload your crash report” micro-tool that returns a plain-English summary and next steps.
  • Community content (Bingo, local sports, giveaways) auto-paired with short educational clips so the brand stays warm while still generating leads.
  • Referral partner drop-box (chiropractors, body shops) that accepts a PDF and returns a chronology.

What This Installs

A production-ready agentic layer that:

  • Improves the existing 7-question tool without increasing friction.
  • Adds voice so leads can talk instead of type.
  • Turns medical records and crash reports into chronologies, gaps, and red flags automatically.
  • Generates consistent short-form content from real case patterns.
  • Runs 24/7 and hands only qualified, structured work to the team.

Built as a concrete work sample for the Principal Applied AI Engineer role — the same capabilities already demonstrated in the live chronology agent on this domain.
