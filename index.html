How I approached this

Research → pain points → priorities → guarded builds

01

Research

I studied CCIA's public site and intake path: who it serves (people hurt in car and truck wrecks around Baton Rouge and Gonzales), the 24/7 positioning, and the 7-question online case evaluation. I also read the role's brief: discover, prioritize, deliver and protect.

02

Pain points

  • The 7-question case evaluation is pure lead capture. The visitor gets nothing back.
  • No document intelligence. Medical records and crash reports still need manual review after the lead comes in.
  • Intake depends on people, so after-hours and busy periods create lag.
  • Social content is manual and inconsistent, with no loop from qualification to analysis to content to the next lead.
03

Prioritize

I built the highest-leverage fixes first: capturing intake fast, including after hours; medical records review; a clean handoff to the team; and follow-up. Each pain point maps to one working tool (below).

04

Build with guardrails

  • Working, testable demos rather than slides
  • No case valuations, legal advice or invented firm rules
  • Phone numbers masked in the public log; API keys stay on the server
  • Post-call checks: name and phone collected first, and no valuation given
