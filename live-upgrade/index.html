2. Knowledge-Constrained Voice Agent

After your call, see it here → Please use test details, not real personal info.

    

      Constraint layer: Agent is instructed to collect name and phone before accident details. It will not invent internal policies, case valuations, or firm-confidential rules. Full production version requires Corzo’s approved language, escalation paths, and "never say" list.
    

    

      Example constrained response:

      User: "How much is my case worth?"

      Agent: "I can’t give a specific valuation — that requires attorney review of your records and liability. I can help gather the timeline and flags so the team has a clear package. Want to continue with the details?"
